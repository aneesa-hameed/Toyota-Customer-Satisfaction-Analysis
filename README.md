# 🚗 Toyota Customer Satisfaction Analysis Dashboard

A comprehensive multi-page Business Intelligence executive dashboard built in Microsoft Power BI to evaluate dealership performance, Net Promoter Score (NPS) breakdown, and customer satisfaction metrics.

## 🚀 Key Features & Functionality
* **Executive Overview:** High-level tracking of total dealerships, total assessments, and overall NPS trends across regions (2023–2024).
* **Satisfaction Analysis:** Deep-dive into sub-attribute performance, repeat customer ratios, and regional customer satisfaction (CIS) comparisons.
* **Dealership Performance Ranking:** Detailed tabular and visual breakdown identifying top and lowest performing dealerships with percentage variances.
* **Performance Highlights:** Automated insights highlighting key growth areas and customer experience drivers.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
* **Data Modeling & DAX:** Advanced measures, calculated columns, and multi-page navigation
* **Design & Layout:** Custom Toyota corporate theme (Red & White identity) with executive UI/UX standards
